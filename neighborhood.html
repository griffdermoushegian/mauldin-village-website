The Neighborhood

North Columbia, without the commute

Mauldin Village sits just off the North Main Street corridor in the 29203 — an established Columbia neighborhood about ten minutes from downtown. Here's what's close.

Columbia College

The historic women's college on Columbia College Drive is minutes away — an anchor of the North Main community since 1854.

Prisma Health Richland

One of the Midlands' largest hospitals is in the neighborhood — a short drive for work or care.

Downtown Columbia

Main Street's restaurants, offices, and the State House are about ten minutes down North Main.

Earlewood Park

One of Columbia's oldest parks — ball fields, trails, a dog park, and the community center — sits between the Village and downtown.

BullStreet District

Segra Park baseball, restaurants, and Columbia's growing BullStreet development are a few minutes south.

Riverfront & USC

Riverfront Park's canal trail and the University of South Carolina are an easy drive across town.
