Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What floor plans does Mauldin Village offer?

Mauldin Village offers three floor plans: a 1 bedroom / 1 bath at 750 sq ft (from $900/mo), a 2 bedroom / 1 bath at 854 sq ft (from $1,095/mo), and a 3 bedroom / 1 bath at 1,052 sq ft (from $1,295/mo). See the Floor Plans page for live availability.

Where is Mauldin Village located?

Mauldin Village is at 5418 Mauldin Avenue, Columbia, SC 29203, in North Columbia — near the North Main Street corridor, about ten minutes from downtown Columbia, and close to Columbia College and Prisma Health Richland.

How do I apply?

Applications are online through our resident portal. Open a listing from the Floor Plans page and select Apply Now. The application fee is $100 per applicant.

Are the homes renovated?

Yes — Braden Fellman Group is renovating Mauldin Village home by home. Contact the leasing team for the current status of specific units and finishes.

When will photos be available?

Community exterior photos are on the Gallery page now. Interior photography is underway as renovations complete — and the leasing team is glad to walk you through available homes in person anytime.

Who manages Mauldin Village?

Braden Fellman Group — a real estate firm that has developed and managed communities in-house since 1981 — owns and manages Mauldin Village.
